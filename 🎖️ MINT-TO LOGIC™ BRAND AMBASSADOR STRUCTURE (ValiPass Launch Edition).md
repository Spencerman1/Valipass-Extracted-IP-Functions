## **📣 2\. AMBASSADOR POSTING & ENGAGEMENT RULES**

* All posts must:

  * Be generated by the assistant (no edits)

  * Use pre-approved imagery

  * Be posted according to optimized schedule (tracked by moderators or Slack reminders)

* Responses to comments must follow **brand tone guidelines**

* Must **log or screenshot posts weekly** (or integrate with platform tracking)

---

## **🤖 3\. ASSISTANT COMMENT-RESPONSE WORKFLOW**

You don’t trust ambassadors to “wing it.”  
 You give them a **governed method** to handle comments using your assistant.

### **🛠 How it works:**

1. Ambassador sees a comment or DM on their post

2. They **copy/paste the comment into the Assistant** under:

   * “Generate Brand Response to Audience Question”

3. Assistant returns a **formatted, on-brand, logic-aligned response**

4. Ambassador pastes reply to their post or sends DM

### **✅ Why this matters:**

* Prevents off-brand replies

* Maintains protocol authority

* Lets ambassadors “speak” Mint-to Logic even if they don’t fully understand it yet

* Evolves ambassadors into educators as they use this feature over time
