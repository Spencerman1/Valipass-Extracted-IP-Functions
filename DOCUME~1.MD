* **Monitor Mint Unit behavior**

* Evaluate **contextual drift** from declared intent

### **2\. Licensing Engine Enforcement at Entry Point:**

The Licensing Engine integrates directly into Layer 3 of the EEC (Ethical Reflection Engine) and Layer 4 (Mint Unit Generation). Its responsibilities include:

* Confirming the actor/protocol has a valid license or standing agreement

* Tagging the issued Mint Unit with **licensing metadata**, including:

  * Scope of use

  * Expiry or revision date

  * Permitted verticals

  * Audit parameters

* Triggering **“Ghost Mode”** for non-licensed requests: actor may enter a read-only replica environment without access to write or deploy functions

**License Enforcement Outcome Matrix:**

| Actor Type | Licensing Status | Result |
| ----- | ----- | ----- |
| Verified | Licensed | Full access granted |
| Verified | Unlicensed | Ghost Mode \+ audit log |
| Spoofed | N/A | Rejected \+ fingerprinted |
| Anonymous | N/A | Sent to Sandbox Review |

**ValiPass™ Nodes** act as the **final gatekeepers** and **distributed checkpoint validators** across the Quantum Superhighway. Each node synchronizes with the EEC in the following ways:

  * ValiPass™ access ledger

These nodes provide **geographic and vertical segmentation** of control without compromising interoperability. ValiPass™ thus functions as the **interstate checkpoint** network of the ecosystem, reinforcing EEC logic at every hop.

### **4\. Failsafe Systems:**

The integration also supports rapid rollback and correction through:

* **Unit Quarantine Protocols (UQPs):** isolate suspicious Mint Units across all synced nodes

* **Audit Trigger Reflex (ATR):** system-wide alert sent to all Reflexive and Licensing Engines when anomalous behavior is detected

* **License Pulse Expiry:** periodic checks ensure no actor exceeds license lifespan or misuses lapsed permissions
